How a film gets made

Five stages, from the first conversation to the finished cut.

01

Discover

We sit with the people who know the story best and work out what actually needs to be said.

02

Shape

A treatment and shot list built around the story's turning points, not a checklist of coverage.

03

Shoot

A small crew, natural light where we can get it, and room to follow what happens on the day.

04

Cut

The edit is where the film is actually written. We cut for rhythm first, polish second.

05

Release

Color, sound and delivery specs handled for wherever the film is going to live.

Behind the camera

One half of the crew, and the reason the studio exists.

DK

Dhruv Kumar

Co-founder & filmmaker

"Story over shots."

Dhruv started KaizenLoom on the belief that a film is judged by what it leaves behind, not by how it looks on a reel. He'd rather cut a beautiful shot than let it slow the story down, and that's the standard the whole studio is built around: narrative and atmosphere first, coverage and polish after.
